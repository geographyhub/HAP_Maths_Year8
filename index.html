How to prepare

  1. Look back through your exercise book at the tasks and questions from your lessons.
  2. Missed a lesson? Ask your teacher, or someone in your class, what you missed and catch up.
  3. Practise each topic on Sparx using the codes above, or review the units on Oak National Academy’s KS3 Maths page.

To get the very top marks

  1. Show your working clearly. You can get marks for your method even if the final answer is wrong.
  2. Check your answers and calculations at the end of the exam.
  3. Practise with your own calculator, the one you will bring to Paper 2.
